# About this directory

**Kathak Vancouver** is a small directory of Kathak artists and teachers. The
community runs it. It covers the Lower Mainland of British Columbia.

The Lower Mainland is large. A teacher in Surrey can be one hour away from a
student in North Vancouver. This site shows you who teaches where. You can then
find a teacher near your home.

## What this site lists

Each entry uses public information. The sources are the website, the school
page, or the social media account of the artist. Each listing gives:

- The main neighborhood or city of the artist.
- The website and the Instagram account, when they exist.
- Whether the artist teaches now.
- A short, factual description.

This site does not list home addresses or personal phone numbers. It lists only
the contact channels that the artist already shares in public.

## Who runs this site

Bibek Shrestha maintains this site. The site is independent of every school and
institution. It is a community resource.

## Updates and corrections

If your information is wrong or missing, write to us. If you prefer no listing,
write to us. We correct or remove the entry the same day. See
[Add yourself](/submit/).
