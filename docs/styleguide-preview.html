A system borrowed from Darbar, in our own colors.

This page is built in the system it documents. Every heading color, radius, button, and reveal on this page is the proposal running live. If the page looks right, the system is right.

What Darbar actually does

Four rules carry the whole site.

One family, light and tight Beatrice carries everything. No serif and sans split. The 40px section heading is weight 400, not bold. Line height ratio is 1.10 at display size and 1.50 at body size.
One hue per section, on the heading only Pink, orange, yellow, indigo. Body text under every one stays #121212. A long page reads as chapters with no rules, boxes, or dividers.
Two radii, no shadows 4px on images and panels. 50px on buttons. Nothing between. Cards separate by whitespace and by an alternating ground.
Word-stagger reveal Headings split into .split-word elements that fade up on scroll. No GSAP, no AOS, no Lenis. About 30 lines of IntersectionObserver.

Color

Brass, alta, indigo, leaf.

Brass is the color of ghungroo bells. Alta is the red dye that dancers apply to the feet and the hands. These are chosen for Kathak, not copied from Darbar.

Brass needed splitting into two tokens. At #E0A012 it fails as text on white, which left the "one hue per section" rule with only two usable heading colors. --brass-ink: #8A5D00 is the text variant. Both are brass.

The rules

#Rule
1All body text is ink. All secondary text is ink muted. An accent never carries a paragraph.
2One accent per section, applied to the section heading only.
3Brass is the only accent allowed on a button.
4A section is either paper or band. Neighboring sections must differ.
5Never place brass fill as text on white. Brass is a fill. Ink sits on top of it.

The current site uses four radii and a two-layer shadow on every card. Two shapes is a system. Five is an accident. Delete --shadow. Cards separate by whitespace and by ground color.

One button rule, two variants

Details and tickets Add yourself

The border is always present and always 1px. On the filled button it matches the fill. On the ghost button the fill drops away and the border keeps the color. Both buttons are therefore the same height and share a baseline. Tab to them to see the focus ring.

The open problem

None of this fixes the missing photographs.

Kathak is a visual art. A directory of Kathak artists that shows no dancer cannot look like a Kathak site. Photography is the load-bearing element on both Darbar pages. Remove it there and almost nothing is left.

First choice

Ask each artist

One photograph and the right to publish it, from every listed artist. The only option that makes the site look like Darbar. It also gives each artist a reason to keep their listing current.

Second choice

One hero, text cards

A single full-bleed photograph at the top, licensed or shot locally. Cards keep the flat accent block. Much of the effect for one permission conversation.

Third choice

Stay text-only

Drop the image slots. Type, color, shape, and rhythm all still change, so the page improves a great deal. It will not look like Darbar.
