Ghungroo Kathak Academy is a school in Surrey. It is affiliated with **Pracheen
Kala Kendra**, one of the best-known classical-arts institutions in India. The
students write certified exams every year.

The academy teaches traditional Kathak. It also teaches Bollywood-Kathak fusion.
It runs online classes for students outside the region.

By enrolment it is one of the largest Kathak schools in the Lower Mainland. It
has several dozen students at different levels.
