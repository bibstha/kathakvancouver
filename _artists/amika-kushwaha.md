Amika performs Kathak with live classical accompaniment. Few Kathak artists in
Canada do this consistently.

She co-curates the **Mushtari Begum Festival of Indian Classical Music and
Dance** with the tabla artist and ghazal singer Cassius Khan. The festival runs
in the Lower Mainland since 2012. It is one of the longest-running platforms for
Indian classical arts in British Columbia.

Amika performs and teaches Kathak across the region. She appeared at Jack Poole
Plaza and at the Massey Theatre.
