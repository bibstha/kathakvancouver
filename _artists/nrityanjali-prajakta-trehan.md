Prajakta Trehan is a Kathak dancer from India. She now lives in Surrey.

She teaches students at her **Nrityanjali Dance School** in the Newton Cultural
Centre. The school ran for several years. Families in South Surrey, Newton, and
Cloverdale can find a teacher near home at this school.

Prajakta performed Kathak in India and in other countries. She performs with
other Kathak artists in the Lower Mainland. She danced duets at the Mushtari
Begum Festival.
