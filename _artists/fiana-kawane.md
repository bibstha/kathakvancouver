Fiana Kawane is a Kathak artist in Vancouver. She has trained in the form for
twenty years. Part of that training was at **Kadamb**, the institute that
Kumudini Lakhia founded in Ahmedabad.

Her recent work takes Kathak out of the theatre. In 2024 she joined the New
Works Share Dance program in the Downtown Eastside, and she led a dance inquiry
project at an elementary school in Hastings-Sunrise through the
Artist-in-Residence Studio program. She has adapted work for outdoor sites with
Dance West, and she has held a residency with Dance Victoria.

She performs and creates work. She does not run public classes.
