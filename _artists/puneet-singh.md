Puneet Singh is a Kathak artist in Surrey. He dances in the **Lucknow
gharana**. He began with Divya Goswami, a student of Munna Shukla. He now
studies under Mahua Shankar, a senior disciple of Birju Maharaj, and he has
taken the Ganda Bandh, the ceremony that formalises that discipleship.

He performs with live musicians, among them the sitarist Gurjit Sikri and the
tabla and pakhawaj player Hriday Buddhdev. In 2025 he danced at the Evergreen
Cultural Centre in Coquitlam and at the Scotiabank Dance Centre in Vancouver,
both in the Traditional India Series of the Bandish Network. He practises at
the Guildford Recreation Centre.

He performs and creates work. He does not run public classes.
