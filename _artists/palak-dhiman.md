Palak is a Kathak artist, educator, and collaborator in Vancouver. She performed
at festivals in Canada, the United States, India, and Nepal.

In 2024 she presented her first full solo at **Place des Arts** in Coquitlam.
She now teaches the first ongoing Kathak course at Place des Arts, for youth and
adults. She also gives private classes and online classes.

Her classroom is warm, respectful, and inclusive. She teaches Kathak as a living
storytelling form, and not only as a sequence of steps. New students from across
the Lower Mainland are welcome.
