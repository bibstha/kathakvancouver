Sri Shakti Academy Vancouver is the British Columbia branch of Sri Shakti
Academy. Ms. Neesha Jhaveri founded the original academy in Hong Kong.

The academy teaches the **Jankiprasad style of the Benaras Gharana** of Kathak.
It is on the North Shore. It runs regular classes, workshops, camps, and
lecture-demonstrations across the Lower Mainland.

The teaching covers technique, theory, and the spiritual lineage of the form.
Families in North Vancouver and West Vancouver can reach the classes without a
bridge crossing.
