TheiTaal Dance Academy teaches **Kathak and Bharatanatyam**. It gives classes
and workshops in Vancouver.

The academy is a community space for both classical Indian forms. Siblings can
study different styles at the same school.

For current class schedules and workshop dates, contact the academy through its
Facebook page.
