Akshaya is a Kathak performer and choreographer. She trained for two decades
under Guru Pt. Mayur Vaidya. She holds a Bachelor's degree in Kathak from Akhil
Bhartiya Gandharva Mahavidyalaya Mandal.

She founded **Akshaya Kathak Academy** in 2023. The academy teaches structured
Kathak courses in Vancouver and Burnaby. The curriculum covers footwork,
abhinaya, theory, and the cultural history of the dance.

Akshaya performed in India and in other countries. In the Lower Mainland she
performed at Vasantotsav, the Surrey Fusion Festival, and Namaste Canada.
