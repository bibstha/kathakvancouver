Alka Goel-Stevens and Shelina Virani founded Kavital in January 1987. It is one
of the longest-running Indian classical dance schools in the Lower Mainland.

The school is in Burnaby. It teaches **Bharata Natyam and Kathak**. The teaching
grounds students in classical technique. It also builds a community around
Indian dance in British Columbia.

The school holds student recitals and graduations.
