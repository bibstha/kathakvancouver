DanceHouse presents the North American premiere of Forbidden, a contemporary
solo based on Kathak, with community partner Indian Summer Festival at the
Vancouver Playhouse, 600 Hamilton Street. Aditi Mangaldas conceived,
choreographed, and performs the work, which explores female desire and the
social taboos that surround it. She trained with Kathak gurus Kumudini Lakhia
and Pandit Birju Maharaj and leads the Aditi Mangaldas Dance Company and the
Drishtikon Dance Foundation in India. The creative team includes composer
Nicki Wells, dramaturge Farooq Chaudhry, lighting designer Michael Hulls, and
costume designer Kimie Nakano. Both performances begin at 8 PM and run for
60 minutes without an intermission, with a pre-show chat at 7:15 PM in the
Upper Lobby each night and a post-show social in the Salon on Friday.
The performance contains flashing lights and mature themes and is recommended
for ages 14 and older. Book through DanceHouse online, by phone at
604-801-6225, or by email at boxoffice@dancehouse.ca.
