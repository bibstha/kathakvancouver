Toronto Kathak artist Tanveer Alam performs at the Roundhouse Performance
Centre, 181 Roundhouse Mews. Alam trained with Sudeshna Maulik, Sandhya Desai,
Pandita Shama Bhate, and Sanjukta Sinha, and now works with Harikishan S. Nair
and Dheerendra Tiwari. Alam has danced at the New York Kathak Festival, Fall
For Dance North, the Aga Khan Museum, and the Vasantotsava Kathak Festival in
New Delhi, and holds the Eldred Family Protégé award from the Ontario Arts
Foundation. Mandala Arts and Culture Society presents the show with the
Roundhouse Community Arts and Recreation Centre. It closes a weekend of Indian
classical dance that opens on Saturday, October 24 at 7:30 PM with
Bharatanatyam by Meera Sreenarayanan. Each artist needs a separate ticket, and
seating is festival seating. For information, write to govindajai@yahoo.com.
