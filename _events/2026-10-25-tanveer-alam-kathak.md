Toronto Kathak artist Tanveer Alam performs at the Roundhouse. Alam trained
with Sudeshna Maulik, Sandhya Desai, Pandita Shama Bhate, and Sanjukta Sinha,
and has danced at the New York Kathak Festival and Fall For Dance North.
Mandala Arts and Culture Society presents the show with the Roundhouse
Community Arts and Recreation Centre. It closes a weekend of Indian classical
dance that opens on Saturday, October 24 with Bharatanatyam by Meera
Sreenarayanan. Each artist needs a separate ticket, and seating is festival
seating.
