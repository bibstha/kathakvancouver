Kathak dancers Gaurav Bhatti and Amrapali Bhandari perform with a live
ensemble. The musicians are Varijashree Venugopal and Dr. Deepak Paramashivan
on vocals, Gurumurthy on tabla, Atish Mukhopadyay on sarod, Shruti Kamath on
sitar, and John Calverley on violin. Praveen D. Rao composed the music, and
Usha Gupta is the artistic director. The programme runs 90 minutes.
