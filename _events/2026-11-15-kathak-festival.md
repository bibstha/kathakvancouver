Nritya Sadan presents a rental-stage festival celebrating the journey of
Kathak dancers. Students and amateur dancers share the stage with
professional Kathak artists, and renowned community musicians bring live
music to the performances. The programme centres on the dancer as Nayika —
the heroine — explored through Nayikas in Taal and Nayikas in Bhaav, where
rhythm, movement, emotion, and storytelling meet. The Surrey Arts Centre is
at 13750 88 Avenue, Surrey. Book through purchase.surrey.ca or the box office
at 604-501-5580, which also arranges accessible seating.
