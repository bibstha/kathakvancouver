Find Kathak teachers and artists near you.

This directory lists the people who teach and perform Kathak across the Lower Mainland: teachers, schools, dancers, and choreographers. Find one near your home in Surrey, Vancouver, Burnaby, or another city in the region. Every listing says whether that artist or school takes students.
