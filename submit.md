# Add yourself, or correct a listing

This directory grows by word of mouth. If you teach or perform Kathak in the
Lower Mainland, send us an email. If your entry needs a correction, send us an
email.

## What to include for a listing

Send these details:

- **Your name**, and your stage name when it is different.
- **Your neighborhood or city**, for example Surrey, Burnaby, or Coquitlam.
- **Your website**, when you have one.
- **Your Instagram handle**, when it is public.
- **Whether you teach**, and the neighborhoods or studios where you teach.
- A **short description** of 3 to 5 sentences. Cover your training, your style,
  and what you offer.
- A link to a public photo that we can use. This item is optional.

## What to include for an event

We list performances, workshops, and intensives in the Lower Mainland. Send
these details:

- **The event title**
- **The date**, and the end date for a multi-day event
- **The start time**
- **The venue** and the neighborhood
- **The presenter**
- **The ticket link, or the information link**
- **The price**, or the word "Free"
- A **short description** of 2 to 3 sentences

An event leaves the homepage on the day after it finishes.

## Where to send it

Email **[hello@kathakvancouver.com](mailto:hello@kathakvancouver.com?subject=Kathak%20Vancouver%20listing)**
with the details above.

We add or correct your listing in a few days. To remove a listing, reply to the
same address. We ask no questions.

## Notes

- Listings are free. This site shows no advertisements, and it takes no payment.
- We list only public contact channels. We never list home addresses or personal
  phone numbers.
- This is a curated directory, and not a marketplace. We list any Kathak artist
  or teacher who practices in the Lower Mainland.
